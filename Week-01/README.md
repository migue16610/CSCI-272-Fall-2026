## What I learned

- A repository is like your own room. You can store your own personal projects with their full line of code and includes your own comments.
- A README file helps people that find your projects, and explains to them how your web projects are formatted, including going more into detail like what are they about, and what to look forward to into your folder.
- A commit record is to help yourself explain the code into small parts.  Its a personalize message explaining how do you interpret what happened in your code, and also acts like a save point.
